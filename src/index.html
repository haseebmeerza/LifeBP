Under 13 years: child BP is judged by age, sex and height percentiles, so adult ranges do not apply. Refer to a paediatrician. [1][2][6]

TestNormalPrediabetes rangeDiabetes range

2 h after mealbelow 140140-199200 or more

Random 200 or more with symptoms (thirst, frequent urination) points to diabetes. Diagnosis needs a lab test, repeated on another day. Low sugar: below 70 treat now, below 54 serious. For people already diagnosed, usual adult targets are 80-130 before meals and below 180 after meals; the doctor sets individual targets. [3][4]
